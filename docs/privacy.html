Federal Funding Finder Privacy

Privacy

Federal Funding Finder has no accounts and stores no personal information. This page matches PRIVACY.md in the repository.

What the server keeps
DataKeptHow long
Accounts, names, emailsNone collectedNone
Your queriesNot logged. Public upstream responses are cached in Netlify Blobs under a SHA-256 hash of the upstream request.Treated as expired after 30 to 60 minutes and deleted on the next read
IP addressCounted by Netlify's edge rate limiter (per IP, 60 requests per minute) and, as a salted hash, in function memory. This server does not store or log the raw IP.Counters reset every 60 seconds
LogsTool name, success or failure, and duration. No query text, no IPs.Netlify function log retention
Upstream terms

Queries are forwarded to public federal APIs, which have their own terms and privacy policies.

  • Grants.gov terms of use and Grants.gov privacy policy
  • NIH RePORTER API notes and usage limits and RePORTER FAQs
  • NSF Award Search API documentation and NSF privacy policy
